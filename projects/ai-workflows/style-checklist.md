# Style checklist

A short checklist for reviewing a topic before technical review. It is the human version of a style checker, and the same rules can be given to an AI assistant or a linting tool.

## Punctuation

- [ ] No long dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or hyphen.
- [ ] No semicolons. Split into two sentences, or use a comma with "and" or "or".

## Word choice

- [ ] No filler such as "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "holistic", or "cutting-edge". Use the plain, specific word.
- [ ] No stacked hedges such as "it is important to note" or "it should be noted". State the point.
- [ ] Terms match the product's official names.

## Headings

- [ ] One level-one heading per file. No skipped levels.
- [ ] Sentence-style capitalization.
- [ ] Task headings start with a verb.
- [ ] No vague headings such as Overview, Details, Information, or Introduction.

## Procedures

- [ ] Purpose is stated in one sentence.
- [ ] Prerequisites come before the steps.
- [ ] One action per step, in active voice.
- [ ] A warning comes before the step it guards.
- [ ] An expected result follows the last step.

## Accuracy

- [ ] Every command, menu path, value, and limit comes from a cited source.
- [ ] Anything unverified is written as a question for the subject-matter expert.
